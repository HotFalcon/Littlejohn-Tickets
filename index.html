What we do

Find the tickets worth buying, before the market does.

Most resellers work from instinct and a few browser tabs. We work from a database. Our software watches the official box-office sites of hundreds of venues and college athletics programs, records every event, price level and open seat, and lines each event up against its listings on the major resale marketplaces.

The result is a ranked list of opportunities: events where demand is outrunning supply, sections that are about to sell out, and seats priced well below what they resell for after fees. We buy where the numbers are clear and list through established resale channels.

The software

From raw inventory to a buying decision.

A pipeline we designed and built ourselves, running on cloud servers without anyone at a keyboard.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Scheduled runs visit each primary ticketing site, walk its full event catalog and read seat-level availability: open, sold, held, and newly released seats.

  2. 02

    Match

    Each event is linked to the same game or show on the resale marketplaces, down to the section, so box-office prices and resale prices can be compared seat for seat.

  3. 03

    Measure

    Resale prices, listing counts and recent sales are recorded every run, giving a history of supply, demand and sell-through for every event we follow.

  4. 04

    Score

    Events and groups of adjacent seats are scored on the spread between cost and expected resale payout after both sides' fees, and on how quickly comparable seats sell.

  5. 05

    Alert

    The best opportunities, seat releases and fast-moving events surface on a dashboard and as alerts, so a decision can be made in minutes, not days.

Advantages

What the software gives us that instinct can't.

Coverage

Hundreds of venues checked several times a day. No person could watch that many box offices, and most opportunities are on sites nobody else is watching.

Speed

Seat releases and price changes are caught on the next run. When held seats go on sale, we know within hours.

Decisions on evidence

Every purchase is backed by real resale prices and sell-through, calculated after marketplace fees, not by a guess about what something might sell for.

History

Raw snapshots of every run are kept, so scores are checked against how events actually played out and improve season over season.

Lower risk

Seeing supply, demand and the full price ladder before buying means fewer tickets bought at the wrong price and fewer left unsold.

It improves itself

AI agents review every run, file and fix problems, and test each change before it ships, so the system gets more reliable every week with little manual upkeep.

How it's built

Engineered like a product, not a script.

The platform is developed through an automated engineering pipeline. Every change goes through a pull request, automated tests, a replay against real data and a review before it reaches production. Experiments run on separate test servers, so production is never put at risk to try something new.

  • Automated test suite on every change
  • Separate test infrastructure for experiments
  • A record and scorecard for every production run
  • Automatic rollback when a change makes a run worse
  • AI agents for code review, run review and fixes
  • Daily audit of scored deals against real sales
